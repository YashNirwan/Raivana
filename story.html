Raivana began with a return

Not just to a place—but to a feeling.

I was born in Rajasthan, where beauty wasn't reserved for grand palaces or museums. It lived quietly in everyday life. In the carved wooden doors of old homes. In the hand-painted walls that had faded with time but never lost their charm. In furniture crafted by artisans whose hands carried skills passed down through generations.

Growing up, I never realized how special it was. It was simply home.

Continue reading

As I grew older and travelled, I noticed something had changed. Homes were becoming more alike. Mass-produced pieces replaced handcrafted ones. Spaces became functional, but they slowly lost the warmth, character, and stories that once made them feel alive.

The craftsmanship I had grown up around was disappearing—not because it lacked beauty, but because people had forgotten to look for it.

That stayed with me.

I kept thinking about the artisans who still carve every detail by hand, who spend days creating something that machines can copy in minutes but never truly recreate. Their work carries patience, heritage, and soul. It deserves more than being tucked away in forgotten workshops.

Raivana was born from the desire to bring those stories back into our homes.

Every piece we create is inspired by Rajasthan's rich artistic heritage and made by skilled artisans who continue to honour traditional craftsmanship. We don't believe furniture is just something you own. We believe it becomes part of your life—holding conversations, celebrations, quiet mornings, and memories that stay long after trends have passed.

Raivana is our way of preserving what we grew up loving while giving it a place in modern homes.

Because some traditions aren't meant to be left behind.

They're meant to be lived with.

Trishna — Founder

From raw earth to finished art

Every Raivana piece is handcrafted in and around Jaipur, in small family-run workshops where every stage is done by hand. No two pieces are ever exactly the same — slight variations in pattern, texture, or finish are proof of the human hand behind them.

    Every brushstroke carries intention

    The artisans we work with have spent decades mastering a single craft. They sit close — closer than you'd expect — leaning into each brushstroke with the kind of focus that comes only from years of practice.

    When you hold a Raivana piece, you are holding the result of someone's absolute best.

    The Raivana promise

    Every order is personally reviewed before it leaves the workshop.

    1. Fair craft practices

    2. Authentic techniques only

    3. Inspected at source
