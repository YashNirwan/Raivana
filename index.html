Our Origin

Not décor.
Devotion
made tangible.

Raivana exists because the world's most beautiful handmade objects are being lost — slowly replaced by machine-made substitutes — while the families who make them struggle for market access.

We source directly from artisan clusters across Rajasthan — Jaipur, Jodhpur, Nathdwara, Pokaran — working with masters whose techniques are passed down through generations. Every object carries not just beauty, but lineage.

The Making

From Earth to Art

A slow, intricate journey — shaped by hand and heritage.

🪨
01
The Clay
Raw earth sourced from Jaipur's surroundings is cleaned and kneaded by hand until perfect consistency. No machines. Pure mineral memory.
🏺
02
Shaping
On a hand-turned wheel, the artisan coaxes the clay into form — plates, cups, vases — each one sculpted with muscle memory built over decades.
🖌️
03
Painting
Using a fine brush and mineral pigments, floral motifs and patterns are traced freehand, from memory. No stencils. No templates.
✨
04
Finished Piece
After glazing and kiln-firing, each piece emerges with its final colour and sheen. Inspected, signed, and ready to travel the world.
⚙️
01
Raw Brass
Sheets of brass sourced from Jaipur's old city foundries — the same alloys used for centuries in Rajasthani temple ware.
🔨
02
Hammering
The Thathera craftsman beats the metal over a mould in rhythmic patterns — a sound that has echoed through these lanes for generations.
🌺
03
Engraving
Fine chisels carve intricate motifs — deities, elephants, florals — into the surface by hand. No laser. No press. Only patience.
✨
04
Finished Piece
Polished to a warm glow, quality-checked, and individually wrapped. Heirloom-grade. Built to outlast generations.
📜
01
The Canvas
Natural fabric or handmade paper primed with chalk and gum arabic — a preparation technique unchanged for 400 years.
✏️
02
Sketching
The master outlines intricate compositions in pencil — scenes of Krishna, peacocks, lotus — all drawn from sacred memory.
🎨
03
Painting in Layers
Natural pigments and gold leaf applied in up to 20 layers over weeks. Each dried before the next. Each stroke deliberate.
🖼️
04
Finished Piece
Signed by the artist and documented with provenance. A painting is not an object — it is a transmission of devotion.
🌿
01
Natural Dyes
Indigo, pomegranate, turmeric, and madder root reduced into vivid dye baths. Natural colour — no synthetics. Hues deepen with age.
🧱
02
Carving the Block
Teak wood blocks hand-carved with floral and geometric patterns — some passed down through three generations of printers in Bagru.
👐
03
Block Printing
The printer stamps the block onto stretched fabric with precise alignment — repeat by repeat — building pattern by hand across metres of cloth.
🌅
04
Finished Piece
Sun-dried on village fields, washed, and hand-finished. Each piece carries the slight imperfection of the human hand — and that's everything.

The Makers

Every brushstroke
has a name
behind it

The women in our artisan network sit in the lanes of Jaipur's old city, painting the same floral motifs their mothers taught them — but now, their work reaches living rooms in London, New York, and Dubai.
