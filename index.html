Not décor. Devotion made tangible.

Raivana exists because the world's most beautiful handmade objects are being lost — slowly replaced by machine-made substitutes — while the families who make them struggle for market access.

We source directly from artisan clusters across Rajasthan — Jaipur, Jodhpur, Nathdwara, Pokaran — working with masters whose techniques are passed down through generations. Every object carries not just beauty, but lineage.

Brass, cast and chased by hand

Every brushstroke has a name behind it

The women in our artisan network sit in the lanes of Jaipur's old city, painting the same floral motifs their mothers taught them — but now, their work reaches living rooms in London, New York, and Dubai.

“When I paint, I am not making a cup. I am making something that will outlive me.”

Raivana ensures each artisan receives transparent pricing and a fair share of every international sale.
